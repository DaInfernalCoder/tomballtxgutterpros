Good questions before gutter work.

Can a gutter cleaning visit include repairs?

Only when the estimate includes them. Debris removal and a flow observation are different from sealing a joint, replacing a hanger, adjusting slope, or changing an outlet. Ask for each repair to be identified separately so you can decide what work to authorize.

How do you decide between five-inch and six-inch gutters?

The roof section, pitch, valleys, run length, outlet location, downspout route, and discharge conditions all matter. A larger channel by itself does not resolve a blocked outlet or restricted downstream path. Ask for the recommendation to be tied to the runs it serves.

Do gutter guards eliminate cleaning?

No. Guards can reduce some debris entering a channel, but material may collect on the cover or pass through, and outlets still need inspection. Product choice should include how the system can be accessed and maintained.

Will new gutters stop every water problem around the home?

No gutter system can guarantee that. Gutters collect roof runoff; roof defects, grading, and underground drainage may require separate evaluation. The estimate should describe the water route the gutter work covers and name any related work that is excluded.
